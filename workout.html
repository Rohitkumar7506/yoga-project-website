Weekly Workout Plan

4-day split for strength, muscle and fat loss.

Chest + Triceps

  • Bench Press — 4×8
  • Incline Dumbbell Press — 3×10
  • Cable Fly — 3×12
  • Tricep Pushdown — 3×12
  • Overhead Extension — 3×10

Back + Biceps

  • Pull-ups — 4×8
  • Barbell Row — 4×8
  • Seated Row — 3×10
  • Bicep Curl — 3×10
  • Hammer Curl — 3×12

Shoulders + Abs

  • Shoulder Press — 4×8
  • Lateral Raise — 4×12
  • Rear Delt Fly — 3×12
  • Leg Raises — 3×12
  • Crunches — 3×15

Legs

  • Squats — 4×8
  • Romanian Deadlift — 4×8
  • Leg Press — 3×10
  • Lunges — 3×12
  • Calf Raises — 4×15

Rest & Recover

Take 2-3 rest days. Sleep and recovery = muscle growth.
